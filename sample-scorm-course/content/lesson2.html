Types of Taxes in Ethiopia

Direct Taxes

Direct taxes are levied on the income or wealth of individuals and businesses:

TaxDescription
Business Profit Tax (BPT)Taxed on net profits of businesses
Personal Income Tax (PIT)Taxed on employment and other income
Rental Income TaxTaxed on income from property rental
Capital Gains TaxTaxed on gains from transfer of assets

Indirect Taxes

Indirect taxes are included in the price of goods and services:

TaxDescription
Value Added Tax (VAT)15% standard rate on most goods/services
Excise TaxLevied on specific goods (alcohol, tobacco, etc.)
Turnover TaxSmall businesses below the VAT threshold
Withholding TaxDeducted at source on payments
