Lesson 1.1

Overview of the Ethiopian Tax System

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Describe the structure of the Ethiopian tax system
  • Identify the key institutions responsible for tax administration
  • Explain the role of the Ministry of Revenue (MoR)

Introduction

Ethiopia operates a federal tax system administered by the federal government and regional states. The Ministry of Revenue is the primary federal institution responsible for collecting taxes and enforcing tax compliance across the country.

The tax system is structured around three pillars: direct taxes (such as income tax), indirect taxes (such as VAT and excise), and customs duties on imports and exports.
